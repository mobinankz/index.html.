3 · Why superposition matters

PrepareA Hadamard gate can turn |0⟩ into an equal superposition.
InterfereQuantum operations change amplitudes, which can reinforce or cancel each other.
MeasureThe algorithm is designed so useful answers become more likely to observe.

Key distinction: superposition is not simply “the qubit is secretly 0 and 1.” The relative phase between amplitudes can affect later interference, which classical probability alone cannot describe.
